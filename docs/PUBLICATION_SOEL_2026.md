## Scope and relationship to this repository

The article presents the SEACS (Self-Evolving Autonomous Control System) research architecture for bounded autonomous control with observation, evaluation, decision selection, execution, and post-action verification. It discusses risk and trust gating, fallback/human review, reproducible fault scenarios, engineering limitations, and possible application domains.

This repository contains **SEACS Lab**, a limited, public, deterministic reference simulator. It is not the complete research implementation and does not claim field validation, operational autonomy, or real-world infrastructure recovery. Its ten-case exact replay is a reproducibility check for the reference model, not an independent production benchmark.

**Figure interpretation:** The article's scenario-comparison and deployment curves should not be cited as independently measured industrial performance unless accompanied by underlying real-world measurement data and methodology.
